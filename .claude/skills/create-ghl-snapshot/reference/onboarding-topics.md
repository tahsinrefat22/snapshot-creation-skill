# Onboarding topics — baseline list

This is the single place to add or remove onboarding topics. Phase 5 starts from this list, drops what the build doesn't use, and adds what the build introduced. Every topic's click path is captured live in Phase 5 — nothing here describes _where_ to click.

Stack assumption: **LC Phone + LC Email** (decision 2026-09-19). If discovery shows a different stack in a given sub-account, the guide follows what's actually there.

| #   | Topic                                                                           | Why the owner must do it                                                                         | Owner needs to have ready                                                                                                         |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0   | Before you start — checklist                                                    | So they don't stall mid-setup                                                                    | (this list, compiled from the rows below)                                                                                         |
| 1   | Log in (web) + install the mobile app                                           | Everything else                                                                                  | login email, password / invite link                                                                                               |
| 2   | Fill in your business details (owner-must-set custom values + business profile) | Site and messages show placeholders until done                                                   | legal name, phone, address, hours, logo file, tagline, licence #, review quotes, social links, policies                           |
| 3   | Get your phone number (LC Phone)                                                | Calls and texts need a number                                                                    | area code preference, or number to port + carrier details                                                                         |
| 4   | Register for texting — A2P 10DLC (LC Phone)                                     | Texts do not send until approved; approval takes time; rejections are common if fields are wrong | EIN / tax ID, legal business name exactly as registered, address, website, business type, sample messages (provided in the guide) |
| 5   | Set up email sending (LC Email; dedicated domain if they own one)               | Emails from workflows need a sending identity                                                    | domain login (if dedicated domain)                                                                                                |
| 6   | Connect your calendar (Google / Outlook)                                        | Two-way sync so bookings don't double-book                                                       | Google/Microsoft login                                                                                                            |
| 7   | Connect Google Business Profile                                                 | Reviews, messaging, and the reviews widget                                                       | Google login that owns the GBP                                                                                                    |
| 8   | Connect Facebook / Instagram                                                    | Lead forms, DMs, ads                                                                             | Facebook login with page admin                                                                                                    |
| 9   | Connect payments (Stripe)                                                       | Deposits, invoices, paid bookings                                                                | Stripe login                                                                                                                      |
| 10  | Add your team; choose who gets notified                                         | Alerts, assignment, calendars                                                                    | names, emails, phones, roles                                                                                                      |
| 11  | Connect your website domain to the funnel/site                                  | So the funnel is at their address                                                                | DNS/registrar login                                                                                                               |
| 12  | Turn on the automations that ship as draft                                      | Some workflows must wait for A2P / number / calendar                                             | (list generated from the ledger, in dependency order)                                                                             |
| 13  | Your 2-minute daily routine (dashboard)                                         | So they actually look                                                                            | —                                                                                                                                 |
| 14  | When a lead comes in — what to do                                               | Per customer type, 3–5 lines                                                                     | —                                                                                                                                 |
