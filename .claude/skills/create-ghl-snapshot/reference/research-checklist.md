# Phase 1 research checklist — what to find out about any business

Everything here is a _question to answer from the web_, not an answer. Cite a URL for each finding or mark it as an assumption.

## A. The business (its own website, GBP, review pages, social)

- [ ] Legal/brand name, location(s), service area
- [ ] Services offered; which are pushed hardest (hero, nav order, ads if visible)
- [ ] Pricing signals: free estimate, price list, financing, membership/maintenance plans
- [ ] Hours; emergency/after-hours availability; how emergencies are handled today
- [ ] How customers contact them today: phone, form (which fields), chat widget, booking tool, text, DM
- [ ] Existing CTAs ("Call now", "Book online", "Get a quote") and where they lead
- [ ] Team size signals: staff page, "family owned", number of trucks/chairs/providers/rooms
- [ ] Reviews: total count, rating, what is praised, what is complained about (speed, communication, price, no-shows, follow-up, cleanliness, results)
- [ ] Automation already visible: auto-replies, booking confirmations, review requests mentioned in reviews
- [ ] Licensing/certification/insurance claims (these often become identity custom values)
- [ ] Guarantees, warranties, policies (cancellation, deposit) — also identity values
- [ ] Brand assets: logo, colours, tagline (for the owner-must-set list, not to hardcode)

## B. The vertical (industry sources, trade associations, survey data)

- [ ] Lead channels and their typical share: calls vs forms vs walk-ins vs referrals vs marketplaces
- [ ] Expected response time; cost of slow response (any stats on lead decay)
- [ ] Missed-call frequency and consequence
- [ ] Decision timeline: same-day (emergency) vs days (quote shopping) vs weeks (large purchase)
- [ ] Average ticket / range; deposit norms
- [ ] Seasonality (record even though seasonal nurture is out of scope for v1)
- [ ] Repeat / maintenance cycles (record for later)
- [ ] No-show rates and what reduces them
- [ ] Review importance in this vertical; where reviews live (Google, Yelp, Healthgrades, Houzz…)
- [ ] Regulatory constraints on messaging (healthcare privacy, financial, cannabis, etc.) that affect what a text may say
- [ ] Typical qualification questions a business in this vertical asks before booking

## C. Customer types — derive from A + B

For each type:

- [ ] Name it by intent, not demographic ("emergency no-heat caller", not "homeowner 35–55")
- [ ] Channel and state on arrival (urgent / researching / returning)
- [ ] What they need, within how long
- [ ] What "handled well" looks like to them
- [ ] What loses them
- [ ] Volume share (estimate, labelled)
- [ ] Trace: which finding in A or B justifies this type

## D. Operating rhythm — reason from evidence, mark inferences

- [ ] Who answers the phone; when nobody does
- [ ] How jobs/appointments are scheduled; who assigns
- [ ] What happens after the job/visit (invoice, follow-up, review ask)
- [ ] What a lost lead looks like today
- [ ] Owner's likely tolerance for automation (do reviews praise a personal touch? keep messages human-sounding)

## E. Pain points → snapshot opportunities

Three to seven bullets, each "evidence → what the snapshot does about it".
